# Pixel Mentor: 3-Minute Demo Script 🎮🪙🧙

Welcome to the official 3-minute evaluation walkthrough for **Pixel Mentor**.

---

### Step 1: Landing Page & Problem Pitch (0:00 - 0:30)
1. Navigate to: `http://localhost:5173/` (or the deployed live URL).
2. **Observe**: Retro 16-bit animated Mentor avatar floating in the hero banner.
3. **Promise**: Learn budgeting, saving, and investing by playing with your own numbers. Education only, zero regulated investment advice.
4. **Action**: Click **"GET STARTED"** to begin onboarding.

---

### Step 2: 60-Second Onboarding Wizard (0:30 - 1:15)
1. **Category**: Select **Gig Worker** (e.g. freelancer, driver, creator).
2. **Cashflow**: Set Monthly Income = `₹45,000` (Irregular income mode).
3. **Fixed Expenses**: Add Rent (`₹12,000`) and Bills (`₹3,000`).
4. **Goal**: Choose **Laptop Upgrade** (`₹60,000`).
5. **Risk Scenarios**: Answer the 4 behavioral questions (e.g. choosing patience over panic).
6. **Result**:
   - Algorithmic scoring assigns: **Balanced Planner** (or **Safe Saver**).
   - Append-only ledger awards: **+100 Coins** onboarding bonus!
   - Automatic redirection to `/dashboard`.

---

### Step 3: Interactive Dashboard & Mentor Analysis Q&A (1:15 - 1:50)
1. **Top Header**: Observe live coin balance counter (`100 🪙`).
2. **Analysis Q&A**: Pixel Mentor pops up with questions tailored to your numbers:
   - *"Your food spending is 35% of income. Is that planned or takeout convenience?"*
   - Tap answer chip: *"Takeout & Zomato"*.
   - Pixel Mentor awards **+25 Coins** for calibrating profile insights!
3. **Budget Tab**:
   - Inspect the **Gig Mode** safety card: see **"Safe to Spend Today"** (`₹850 / Day`).
   - Log an expense: ₹250 for "Evening coffee".
   - Charts update inline without page reload, granting **+5 Coins**.

---

### Step 4: What If Time Machine & Compounding (1:50 - 2:20)
1. Click **Goals / Time Machine** tab.
2. Drag the **Monthly SIP** slider from ₹2,000 to ₹5,000.
3. Toggle lifestyle tweaks: *"Skip 2 delivery orders/month"* (+₹800/mo).
4. Watch the Recharts curve update in real time with projected date: *"At this rate, you reach your Coding Laptop goal in 11 months!"*

---

### Step 5: Scam Shield Live Scanner (2:20 - 2:40)
1. Click **Scam Shield** tab.
2. Click **Sample #1 (VIP Crypto Bot)**:
   - Message: *"Join our secret VIP Telegram group! 100% guaranteed 25% weekly returns..."*
3. Click **"RUN SCAM SHIELD SCANNER"**:
   - Animated risk meter rises to **95/100 (DANGER)**.
   - Exact red-flag phrases are highlighted in red.
   - Mentor explains the trap and suggests SEBI-regulated index funds.

---

### Step 6: Arcade Game & Mistake Replay (2:40 - 3:00)
1. Click **Arcade Games** tab.
2. Select **Crash Survivor** (Entry: 20 🪙).
3. Check *"I understand simulation rules"* and enter.
4. **Crisis**: Headlines scream panic! The market drops -30%.
5. Click **"BUY THE DIP"** (or "Panic Sell").
6. **Mistake Replay Screen**:
   - View your decision path vs the optimal recovery curve.
   - Learn why emotional panic sells guarantee loss, while patience captures recoveries.
   - Earn win reward coins!
